Privacy

NBA Player Lab has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and sets no cookies. Your theme, language and last player are kept in your own browser only.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. Player pictures and club logos load from NBA's servers (cdn.nba.com), which see your IP address as any website would.

Private fantasy league

With its members' agreement, one private Yahoo Fantasy league can have its own page here. A league member reads it from Yahoo with read-only access, at most a few times a day.

The league page is encrypted and opens only with the invite link given to the league's members. It is replaced at least every 24 hours and never kept in the site's code or its history. Yahoo account ids and email addresses are never stored or shown.

Any member can ask for the league page to be removed, and it is taken down with the next update.
